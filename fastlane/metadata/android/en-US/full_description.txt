TouchPause adds a simple Quick Settings tile that temporarily pauses touchscreen input without stopping the app, video, or other content already on screen.

Use it while cleaning your screen, tracing an image, presenting something without accidental taps, or keeping controls untouched while media continues to play.

HOW IT WORKS

• Add the TouchPause tile to Quick Settings.
• Choose Volume Up or Volume Down as the physical release key.
• Tap the tile to pause touch.
• Press the selected release key to restore touch at any time.

TOUCH CONTROL ON ANDROID 14+

TouchPause uses a user-enabled Android Accessibility service to pause touchscreen and stylus motion without root. Only during an active pause, the service receives touchscreen and stylus motion, including positions and timing, and filters hardware keys. It receives all hardware-key events only to detect the selected volume key and forwards unrelated keys. All events are processed only on the device and discarded immediately. TouchPause never reads screen content and never stores or shares touch, stylus, or key events.

TouchPause requires Android 14 or newer and uses Volume Up or Volume Down as the release key. Touch exploration or another service that filters hardware keys can make touch pausing unavailable. The app never requests root access.
